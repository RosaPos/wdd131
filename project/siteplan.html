Site Name

Site Name: 3D XD

Why this name? “3D XD” is short, memorable, and matches the brand of a small custom 3D printing business. It represents creativity and fun (XD) while clearly indicating the service (3D printing).

Domain idea: 3dxd-printing.com or 3dxd.mx

Site Purpose

The purpose of the 3D XD website is to present and promote a small custom 3D printing business. The site will inform visitors about the products and services offered, showcase examples of 3D printed items, and provide an easy way for customers to contact the business.

The website will include information about personalized 3D printed products such as keychains, educational items, gifts, and event souvenirs. It will also allow potential customers to request information or place inquiries through a contact form.

Scenarios

These are real questions a visitor may ask.

  • What types of custom 3D printed products does 3D XD offer for school or children’s events?
  • How can I request a personalized 3D printed item or get a price quote?
  • Are the 3D printed products suitable for gifts, parties, or special events?

Color Schema

The color scheme for the 3D XD website is designed to reflect technology, creativity, and professionalism.

Header, navigation, footer, buttons, strong accents.

White #FFFFFF

Page background for clarity and accessibility.

Light Gray #F5F5F5

Cards/sections background for separation.

Typography
